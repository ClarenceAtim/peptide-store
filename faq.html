Frequently asked questions

Answers about ordering, paying, delivery, and what these products are for. Anything marked “to be confirmed by the store” is still being set by the store.

Do I need an account to buy?

No. You can buy as a guest — no account, no login, and no password is ever required to place an order. Your cart is saved in your own browser only.

How do I pay for my order?

Placing an order sends an order request. No payment is taken on this site. The store emails you payment details (bank transfer or crypto) after you place the order, and your order is confirmed once payment has been received.

Where do you deliver?

Delivery is across Europe. You enter a European delivery address at guest checkout. Shipping costs are confirmed by email when your order is confirmed.

How long will my order take?

Delivery time depends on the destination and on availability, and is confirmed by email when your order is confirmed. Exact delivery times are to be confirmed by the store — no delivery time is promised on this page.

What are these products for?

All products are for laboratory research use only. They are not for human or animal use. They are not drugs, foods, dietary supplements, or cosmetics, and they are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease.

How do I check on my order?

You receive an order reference (starting PEP-2026-) on screen after placing an order request. Keep it. There is no live tracking page yet — to ask about your order, reply to your order confirmation email quoting your reference. Live order tracking is to be confirmed by the store.

Can I return a product?

Returns are handled by the store on a case-by-case basis. Contact the store quoting your order reference. The returns policy is to be confirmed by the store.

Why are prices in US dollars?

Prices are shown in US dollars (USD) for now. The store will set euro (EUR) pricing.

Are the photos of the actual products?

Product photos show vials of the listed products. The store will replace them with its own product photographs.
