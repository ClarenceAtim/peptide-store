Privacy notice

Last updated: November 2026.

What we collect

When you place an order, we ask for your name, email address, delivery address, and optionally your phone number and delivery notes. We need these details to confirm your order, send you payment details, and deliver your order.

What we do not collect

There is no account on this site, so we do not store usernames or passwords. No payment details are collected on this site — payment is arranged by email after you place an order request. Your cart is stored in your own browser (local storage) and is not sent to the store until you place an order.

How your details are used

  • To confirm your order and send payment details by email.
  • To arrange delivery to the address you gave.
  • To reply to you about your order.

Your details are not sold and are not used for marketing unless you ask for that separately.

Your rights

You can ask the store what details it holds about your order, ask for a correction, or ask for your details to be deleted once your order is complete. Reply to your order confirmation email, quoting your order reference.

Contact

For privacy questions, reply to the order confirmation email you receive, quoting your order reference.
